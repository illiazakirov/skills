# Brand Wheel

Apple, 1997. A teaching reconstruction.

## Attributes

- One-piece design
- Its own operating system
- Color and translucency
- Premium price

## Functional benefits

- Works out of the box
- Easy to learn
- Fewer things to go wrong

## Emotional benefits

- I feel creative
- I feel capable
- I am one of the people who think differently

## Values

- Creativity
- Simplicity
- Nonconformity

## Personality

- Rebellious: takes the side of the misfit against the default choice.
- Imaginative: shows what could be made, not specifications.
- Plain-spoken: short words, no jargon.
- Confident: says a thing once.

## Essence

Think different.

## Note

The essence is the line of Apple's 1997 campaign; here the tagline and the essence happen to be the same words, which is rare. Everything else is our wording.
