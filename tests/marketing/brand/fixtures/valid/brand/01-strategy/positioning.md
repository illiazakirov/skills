# Positioning

Apple iMac, 1998. A teaching reconstruction.

## Competitive alternatives

- A Windows PC from Compaq, Dell or Gateway.
- Putting off buying a computer.

## Unique attributes

- Computer and screen in one piece.
- Hardware and operating system from one maker.
- Designed as an object: translucent, colored.

## Value

- Out of the box and online in minutes.
- Fewer things to set up and to go wrong.
- A computer for the living room, not one to hide under a desk.

## Target segment

First-time home buyers who want the internet, not a hobby.

## Market category

Home computer.

## Positioning statement

For people who want to get online without becoming computer experts, iMac is the home computer that works straight out of the box. Unlike a Windows PC, it is one piece, designed and built by one company.
