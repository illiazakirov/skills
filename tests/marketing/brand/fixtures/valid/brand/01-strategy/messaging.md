# Messaging

Apple iMac, 1998. A teaching reconstruction.

## Main promise

A computer that gets out of the way of your ideas.

## Proofs

- Online in minutes: plug in the power and the phone line; there is no third step.
- One company makes all of it: Apple designs the computer and its operating system together.
- The tool of people who make things: design studios and publishers already run on Macs.

## Objections

- "It will not run the software I need." Microsoft Office and Internet Explorer are made for the Mac.
- "Apple may not exist next year." Apple has been profitable again since 1998.
- "It costs more than a PC." At $1,299 the screen, the modem and the software are all in the box.

## Note

The promise is our wording. The proofs and answers rest on facts: the iMac advertising said "There's no step 3", Microsoft committed to Office for the Mac in 1997, and Apple's 1998 financial year was its first profitable one since 1995.
