**Personality**:

### Voice

File: `02-brand-platform/voice.md`

**Voice**: We sound like a friend who knows computers, never like a manual.

**Tone**:

- Formal–casual: 4 of 5. One person talking to another across a table.
- Serious–funny: 3 of 5. Wit in the headline, straight in the facts.
- Respectful–irreverent: 4 of 5. Mocks the industry, never the customer.
- Matter-of-fact–enthusiastic: 4 of 5. Excited about what you can do, not about the machine.

**We write like this**:

- Short sentences, one idea each.
- What it lets you do comes before what is inside.
- A headline may play with words.
- Speak to "you".

**We never write**:

- Megahertz or model numbers in a headline.
- Words only engineers use.
- A sentence that needs a second reading.

**Examples**:

- Headline. Right: "Chic. Not geek." Wrong: "The new all-in-one G3 desktop, now at 233 MHz."
- Call to action. Right: "Think different." Wrong: "Compare our specifications."
- Answer to an objection (it is hard to set up). Right: "Step one: Plug in. Step two: Get connected. Step three: There is no step three." Wrong: "Setup requires only minimal configuration."
- Refusal (no beige model). Right: "Sorry, no beige." Wrong: "This model is not available in other finishes."

## How to use this

**Read first.** This book, from the start. The strategy says what the brand promises and to whom; the platform says how it sounds; the identity and the design system say how it looks.

**Fixed.** The logo files, the palette, the two families and their sizes, the motto, and every value in `tokens.json`. Use them as they are. Never redraw the logo, add a color or set a size by hand.

**Free.** The composition of a page, the images, the length of a text, the order of sections, within the rules of this book.

**Check before you ship.**

- Text reaches 4.5 to 1 on its background, and the borders of controls 3 to 1, in both themes.
- No line breaks a rule of We never write.
- The accent appears only where Accent is for allows it.
- The logo is never smaller than its Minimum size and keeps its Clear space.

**When something is missing.** Do not improvise. Name what is missing and go back to the step that owns it: the strategy, the platform, the identity or the design system.
