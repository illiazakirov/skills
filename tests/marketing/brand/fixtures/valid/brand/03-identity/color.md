# Color

## Color idea

White, and one color you can see through.

## Palette

- White - #FFFFFF - background on light, text on dark
- Black - #000000 - text on light, background on dark
- Bondi Blue - #0095B6 - accent

## Accent is for

The product, and one detail on the page that points to it.

## Accent is never for

Body text, backgrounds, or a second accent beside it.

## Contrast

- Black on White: 21 to 1
- White on Black: 21 to 1
- Bondi Blue on White: 3.5 to 1, large text only
- Bondi Blue on Black: 6 to 1
