# Interface

## Interface idea

Controls you want to touch: liquid, lit from above, never flat.

## Shape

Controls 22 px, cards 14 px, badges fully round.

## Depth

Raised: soft shadows under controls and cards, hairline borders on fields.

## Density

8 px unit, 44 px controls

## Motion

180 ms, eased out; a pressed button sinks one pixel.

## Never

- A square button.
- A shadow under text.
- Two raised controls side by side in the accent.

## Rationale

Fixture: the Aqua interface of Mac OS X, 2000, reduced to the slots.
