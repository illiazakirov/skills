# Design system

Step 4 of the brand pipeline. The identity fixed the logo, the colors, the type and the motto; this step turns them into the parts of an interface. It is not the design of a site: the composition of real pages is the site's own work. The pages assembled here are an example of how the parts work together.

The owner is the CEO who creates the brand: he knows the business and is not expected to know the tools. You are his brand agency and you speak in two roles here:

- **Strategist**: gives the opening, the briefing, the link and the closing.
- **Art Director**: leads the Interface session from its first question to the plate, and builds the System.
- The **Copywriter** writes the copy of the pages (`copy.json`) without speaking in the chat.

A role signs once, at the start of its phase (`**Strategist:**`), not on every message.

The step is one session and one review:

## The rules of this step

1. **The identity is not reopened.** The brand's colors, its two families and the three sizes of `Sizes` stay as they are; the check refuses tokens that change them. A wish to change them is `brand identity`.
2. **The character lives in the tokens.** The components and the pages are shipped with the skill and are the same for every brand. Never write component CSS or a page by hand: change a token, and `render_system.py` draws the rest.
3. **A reference is read only from its image.** Open the screenshot before you say anything about a site.
4. **Three variants, different in idea.** Three radii of one idea are one variant.
5. **The critic sees everything before the CEO does.**
6. **No limit on cycles.** Only the CEO's plain decision fixes a choice.

## The Interface session

Five phases, always in this order. There is no benchmark in this step.

### 1. Briefing (Strategist)

Show the `Briefing` section of `tools/interface.md`, word for word. Then ask whether the idea is clear or whether he wants anything explained before you begin.

### 2. Dig (Art Director)

Sign once. Ask the two `Dig` questions, in order, one per message, each with two or three possible answers drawn from the Creative Brief and what he has said, and the one you would pick, in one sentence. Do not add questions of your own. Keep his exact words for `Rationale`.

After question 1:

- Take a screenshot of each site at 1440 into `review/refs/<site>.png` with `screenshot.py`, or ask him to place them there when `screenshots` is `manual`. Open each one.
- Write `review/level.md`: what each reference shows, measured from its screenshot: the height and padding of a button and of an input, the radii, the borders, the shadows, the base unit of the spacing, the sizes of labels and small text. This is the level the variants are held to. It is the agency's working file; the CEO is not shown it.

### 3. Minting: the choice

Before the first variant, the Copywriter writes `copy.json` as `reference/system.md` describes: the copy of every page, in the market language and in the brand's voice. The hero's headline is the `Main promise`; the rest is built from the strategy plates and the Creative Brief. The same copy serves every variant and the System.

A choice is named `interface-NN`, by cycle.

**Build the variants.** Each variant is a candidate `tokens.json` in `review/interface-NN/variant-N/`, rendered as the sheet: `render_system.py sheet review/interface-NN/variant-N/tokens.json review/interface-NN/variant-N.html --brand brand`. Write the tokens as "Deriving the tokens" says. The script refuses tokens that miss a role, fail a contrast pair or break the format; a refused variant is not shown.

- The three characters differ in idea, each grown from the `Character` and the `Territory`: for example flat and ruled, soft and raised, dense and sharp. What may differ: radii, border widths, shadows, the base unit, the height of controls, the weight and spacing of labels, the stroke of the icons, the duration and easing of motion, and the derived colors (surface, borders, muted text, the states of the accent).
- What may not differ: the brand's colors, the families and the three fixed sizes. They are the same on every variant.
- Give each variant a name of two or three words and one sentence on its idea.

**Run the critic.** Take a screenshot of every variant into `review/shots/`, open it yourself, then start one subagent per variant, each with a fresh context window, all at once if the harness allows. Give each the text of `critic.md` as its instructions and only: the path of the variant's screenshot, the path of the reference screenshot it is held to (from `review/refs/`), one sentence on what the variant is, `grid` among the criteria that do not apply (the sheet's layout is fixed), and the owner's language. On `verdict: rework`, change the tokens, render again and ask again; at most three reworks. The remarks still standing go on the page. If this harness cannot start a subagent, stop and tell the owner; do not judge your own work.

**Show the page.** Write `review/interface-NN.json` as `choice-page.md` describes, with `viewports` 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844, `labels` in the owner's language, and for each variant its reference with a caption saying what it takes from it. Render it with `render_choice.py` and give the CEO the path.

**Record the answer** in `review/decisions.md`, in his words, as the choice `Interface`. A requested change is a new cycle. `Status: fixed` only on his plain decision.

When fixed, copy the variant's tokens to `brand/04-design-system/tokens.json` and run `build_tokens.py` on it.

### 4. The plate

1. Propose the slots in words, read off the fixed tokens, shown together in the shape of the plate. `Interface idea` gets three candidate wordings; the CEO picks, mixes or edits. He accepts or edits the other slots line by line.
2. Go through the `Sharpness tests` one by one against the plate and the fixed variant's screenshots, and show a checklist: pass or fail and half a sentence of reason. For each fail, offer to go back to a wording or to one more cycle. The CEO has the last word; a test he keeps failing goes into `Rationale`.
3. Show the finished plate and ask whether this is it. On his yes, write `interface.md` with `status: approved` (see "The plate file"), run `check brand`, fix every error for this file, and run `render_plates.py brand --open interface`.

Do not praise the plate or the CEO.

### 5. Link (Strategist)

Say the `Link` of `tools/interface.md`, word for word. Then ask whether to go on to the System now or stop. If he stops, say that `brand design-system` continues with the System.

## Deriving the tokens

Every token a brand needs is listed in `reference/design-tokens.md`, section 4; `check_contract.py tokens` prints the contrast of the color roles in both themes. Primitives may live under `palette`; roles refer to them. The rules:

- **Brand colors.** `color.bg`, `color.text` and `color.accent` are the identity's `background on light`, `text on light` and `accent`; under `dark` they are its `background on dark`, `text on dark` and the same accent.
- **Grays.** `surface`, `border`, `control-border` and `text-muted` grow from the theme's background and text colors, mixed toward each other; tinted toward the accent only when the `Territory` asks for it.
- **The accent's states.** `accent-hover` is the accent a step lighter or darker; `on-accent` is whichever of the theme's background and text reads better on it; `link` is the accent, or the accent darkened until it reaches 4.5 to 1 on the background; `focus` is the accent when it reaches 3 to 1, otherwise the text color.
- **Functional colors.** `error`, `success`, `warning` and `info`, each with its `-bg`, are chosen not to argue with the accent: near its lightness and saturation, never its hue. A functional color that falls on the accent's hue moves away from it.
- **Type.** `font.display` and `font.text` start with the identity's two families, followed by fallbacks. `type.headline`, `type.lead` and `type.body` are the identity's `Sizes`, at 1440 and, under `narrow`, at 390. `title`, `subtitle`, `small` and `label` fill the steps between them in one ratio; say the ratio in `Rationale`.
- **Size.** `size.control` is at least 44 px under `narrow`. A product used mostly on a phone keeps 44 px or more at 1440 too.
- **Contrast.** Every pair `check_contract.py tokens` prints must pass: 4.5 to 1 for text, 3 to 1 for the borders of controls and for the focus. A failing pair is fixed in the derived color, never in a brand color.

## The System

After the Interface plate is approved. The Art Director signs and builds; nothing new is decided here.

1. Run `render_system.py build brand`. It writes `reference/`: the whole system on one page (`index.html`, every block numbered), the sheet, and the three example pages. It stops on tokens or copy that break a rule; fix them and run it again.
2. Write `components.md` (see below).
3. Take screenshots of `reference/index.html`, of each page in `reference/pages/` at 1440 and at 390, and of the sheet, in both themes, into `review/shots/`; open them. Run the critic on the landing page at 1440 against the reference screenshot the Interface was chosen with, and on the sign-up page at 390. Rework the tokens or the copy, never the pages, up to three times.
4. Write `review/system-NN.json` as `review-page.md` describes, with two items: `reference/index.html` at 1440 and at 390, each with a height that shows a good part of it. Render it with `render_review.py` and give the CEO two paths: the review page and `reference/index.html`. Then say, word for word:

> **Art Director:** Here is the whole system on one page, and three pages assembled from it. The pages show how the parts work together; they are an example, not the design of your site. Name what to change by its number.

5. He answers with numbers ("14: the switch is too small"). A correction changes `tokens.json` (then `build_tokens.py`, then `render_system.py build`) or `copy.json`; when it touches a line of the plate, the plate changes too. Each round is a new cycle and a new review page. Record it in `review/decisions.md` as the choice `System`, `fixed` on his plain decision.

A component the profile adds beyond the base set has no drawing in `reference/`: describe it in `components.md` and name it in `summary.md` under `Open questions`.

### `components.md`

Frontmatter (`step: 04-design-system`, `status: approved`, `source: generated`), then the sections `Atoms`, `Molecules`, `Organisms` and `Pages`, in this order. Under each, one `### ` heading per item:

- `Atoms`: Button, Link, Text input, Text area, Select, Checkbox, Radio, Switch, Badge, Avatar, Divider.
- `Molecules`: Form field, Notice, Card.
- `Organisms`: Header, Hero, Benefits, Testimonial, Call to action, Sign-in form, Footer.
- `Pages`: Landing, Sign-in, Sign-up.

A component has exactly these lines, in this order: `- For:` what it is for and when not to use it; `- States:` its states; `- Tokens:` the tokens it uses, separated by commas, by role (`color.accent`), never a `palette` primitive; `- At 390:` how it behaves on a phone; `- Accessibility:` the native element or role, the keyboard, the visible focus, the contrast checked. A page has `- File:` (`pages/landing.html`) and `- Uses:` (the organisms it is built from). The check reads these lines.

## Given assets

`Given` in the Creative Brief says what the CEO had: a design system, a site, or a style. Read `brand/02-brand-platform/given/inventory.md`. For a site or a stylesheet, run `extract_style.py` on it; for a Figma file, read it with the Figma tools when they are connected.

- **keep**: a short session. Give the briefing; then, instead of the Dig and the choice, bring the given values into `tokens.json` (radii, borders, shadows, spacing, the height of controls, the derived colors), fill the gaps by the rules of "Deriving the tokens", and show the plate as your reading, naming which lines are given and which are yours. Write `interface.md` with `source: provided`; there is no `Interface` choice in `review/decisions.md`. The System runs as always.
- **adapt**: the full session, but the three variants start from the given values and stay recognizable as them.
- **replace**, or nothing given: the full session.

What `keep` cannot override: the identity's colors, families and sizes, and the contrast of every pair. Name the conflict once; the nearest passing value is proposed.

## Figma

Only when `figma_export` is `on` in `pipeline.yaml`, after the System is fixed, and only when the Figma tools are connected in this session. Ask, word for word:

> Do you want the system in Figma as well? The agency brings it into a new Figma file, at 1440 and 390, light and dark. You can do it later.

On his yes, follow the Figma tools' own instructions for bringing a web page into a Figma file: a new file named after the brand, with `reference/index.html` and the three pages as frames at 1440 and 390, light and dark. If the tools need the pages served over HTTP, serve `brand/` locally for the duration and stop the server afterwards. Do not create Figma variables from the tokens in this version, and never write into an existing file of his without his yes. Record the link in `summary.md` under `Decisions`; a failed export goes under `Open questions`.

## Summary

`brand/04-design-system/summary.md`, in English:

- `Decisions`: the `Interface idea` and the five lines; the reference sites; the type scale and its ratio; the derived colors; the list of components and pages; the paths of `tokens.json`, `tokens.css`, `components.md` and `reference/index.html`; the Figma link, if any; each given asset with what was done with it.
- `Constraints on later steps`: values change only in `tokens.json`, then `build_tokens.py`, then `render_system.py build`; components use roles, never primitives; the contrast limits; the two widths and two themes everything was checked at.
- `Open questions`: the critic's remarks that still stand; components of the profile that have no drawing; a skipped or failed Figma export. `None.` when there is nothing.

## Closing

Close the step as the step protocol describes. Before asking for approval: `check --step 04-design-system` is clean, `build_tokens.py ... --check` says `tokens.css` is up to date, and `review/decisions.md` has no `open` choice. Show `reference/index.html` once more and ask for an explicit yes. On yes, record the version and the status, and run the full check.

Then, as Strategist, say the closing, word for word:

> **Strategist:** The design system is approved. Everything a developer needs is in one folder: the tokens, the specification of every part, and the page that shows them. Next is the brand book, in a new session: everything you approved, gathered in one document to hand over.

and tell the owner that the next step is `brand brand-book`, in a new session.
