# Mood

Fixed text of session 4 of 4 of the brand platform. Show `Briefing` word for word; ask the `Dig` questions as written; show the `Sharpness tests` as a checklist; say the `Link` as written. When the brand already has a name there is no Name session: say "of 3" instead of "of 4", and this session is 3 of 3.

## Briefing

**Strategist:** Session 4 of 4: the **Mood**.

**Where it comes from.** The moodboard: studio practice in fashion, interiors and advertising, no single author.

**The idea.** Before anyone reads a word, they have already felt something. A mood territory names that feeling and fences it: what the brand takes, and what it refuses. It is not a logo, a color or a typeface. Those are chosen later, inside the fence.

**Why now.** The character, the voice and the name are decided in words. The identity needs them turned into something an eye can judge.

**What we will leave with.** A board of six to twelve images, each with a line on what is taken from it, and the headline: the **Territory**, two to four words.

From here the Art Director leads.

## Opening

**Art Director:** Is anything ready already: colors, fonts, a logo, a design system, a site? For each thing you have, tell me: keep it as it is, adapt it, or replace it.

(only when something is left to explore)
How shall I make the studies: generated images, which cost money and need a budget from you; drawn studies, which are free; or real references only? And the screenshots of sites: shall I take them, or will you put them in the folder?

## Dig

1. Name three brands, places or objects, from any field, that feel the way yours should.
2. Name three it must never be mistaken for.
3. (for each one named) What exactly draws you to it, or pushes you away?
4. Where will people meet the brand first: a phone screen, a shop, a package, a stage?
5. What should a person feel in the first three seconds, before reading anything?

## Sharpness tests

- Each mood word rules something out.
- Every image on the board has a line that says what is taken from it.
- Each line in Excludes is something a competitor really looks like.
- A designer could turn a proposal down with this plate alone.
- The Character would live here.

## Link

Next: the Creative Brief. The plates of this step become one page, and the identity is drawn from that page alone.

## Notes for the agency

- The Strategist gives the briefing and the benchmark. From `Opening` on, the Art Director leads and signs once: `**Art Director:**`.
- Ask the second opening question only when something is left to explore.
- Ask question 3 once for every brand, place or object the CEO named in questions 1 and 2. Keep his words: they become the lines under the images of the board.
- Keep the answer to question 5 in `Rationale`, as one line that starts "First three seconds:". The Creative Brief copies it from there.
- The minting of this session is the choice of a territory, as the skill describes; the Art Director proposes, there is no Copywriter here.
- `Territory` is 2 to 4 words.
- `Mood words` is exactly three single words.
- `Takes` is 3 to 5 lines; `Excludes` is exactly three.
- The headline slot is `Territory`.
