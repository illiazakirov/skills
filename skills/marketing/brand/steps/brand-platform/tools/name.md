# Name

Fixed text of session 3 of 4 of the brand platform. Show `Briefing` word for word; ask the `Dig` questions as written; show the `Sharpness tests` as a checklist; say the `Link` as written.

## Briefing

**Strategist:** Session 3 of 4: the **Name**.

**Where it comes from.** Agency practice, no single author.

**The idea.** The name is the shortest thing the brand will ever say. It can work in three ways: say what the thing is, borrow a picture from somewhere else, or be a new word that means nothing until you fill it. Whichever way, it must be easy to say, possible to spell after hearing it once, and free to own.

**Why now.** A name chosen before the character is a label. Now there is a character with a voice, and the name can be cast for it.

**What we will leave with.** The **Name**, its domain, and one line on what the name says.

## Dig

1. In which languages must people say and spell the name without help?
2. What should a stranger guess from the name alone, and what must they never guess?
3. Which names, in any field, do you admire, and which would you be embarrassed to carry?
4. Will the brand outgrow what it sells today? A name that describes the product stays with the product.
5. Which domain zones matter, and must the domain match the name exactly?

## Sharpness tests

- Someone who hears it once can spell it.
- It is not the name of an alternative from the Positioning plate, and not one letter away from one.
- The domain was checked, not assumed.
- The Character could carry this name.
- It still fits if the product changes.

## Link

Next: the Mood. The brand has a name and a voice; next we find what it feels like before a single word is read.

## Notes for the agency

- This session runs only when the Brief says `Unnamed`.
- The Dig gives the naming brief: the languages, what the name should and must not suggest, whether it must outlive the product, the domain zones.
- Before the first candidates, say once that the agency does not check trademarks.
- The minting is in rounds. A round is nine candidates, three per approach: descriptive, metaphor, invented. A profile may add a fourth approach. Each candidate has one line of reasoning from the Character and the Voice. Candidates are in the market language.
- The CEO keeps up to three finalists. Only for them: check the domain in each zone he named with `check_domains.py`, and compare the name with the alternatives of the Positioning plate. Rounds repeat until he chooses.
- `Name` is the name itself, up to 3 words.
- `Domain` is one line. Write "not registered" as "likely free; confirm with a registrar", and an unknown answer as unknown, never as free.
- `What it says` is one line of up to 15 words.
- `Rejected` is optional: up to three finalists that lost, each "Name: reason".
- After the plate is approved, write the name into `Brand` of The Brief, as the skill describes.
- The headline slot is `Name`.
