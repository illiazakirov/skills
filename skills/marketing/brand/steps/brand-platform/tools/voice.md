# Voice

Fixed text of session 2 of 4 of the brand platform. Show `Briefing` word for word; ask the `Dig` questions as written; show the `Sharpness tests` as a checklist; say the `Link` as written. When the brand already has a name there is no Name session: say "of 3" instead of "of 4".

## Briefing

**Strategist:** Session 2 of 4: the **Voice**.

**Where it comes from.** The four dimensions of tone of voice, Nielsen Norman Group, 2016.

**The idea.** A voice is a position on four scales: formal or casual, serious or funny, respectful or irreverent, matter-of-fact or enthusiastic. The voice stays the same everywhere; only its volume changes with the situation. A voice is written down as rules that one sentence can be checked against, and proven by examples.

**Why now.** The character has a face. Before it gets a look, it has to speak: the name, the tagline and every page from here on are written in this voice.

**What we will leave with.** Four positions, the rules, four examples in the language of your market, and the headline: the **Voice**, one line in the form "We sound like ..., never like ...".

## Dig

1. Show me something you wrote that sounds like the brand at its best: a post, a letter, a page.
2. Whose writing do you envy, and whose can you not stand?
3. Which words does your customer use for the problem, and which words of your industry do they never use?
4. When you have to say no or bring bad news, how do you say it?
5. Would your customer be surprised to hear a joke from you?

## Sharpness tests

- Reading the four "Right" examples, you would guess one writer.
- Each rule can be checked against a single sentence.
- Each "Wrong" example is something a real competitor could publish.
- No scale sits in the middle without a reason.
- This is how the Character would talk.

- `Voice` is one line of up to 15 words in the form "We sound like ..., never like ...".
- `Tone` is four lines, one per scale, in the order of the briefing: `Formal–casual`, `Serious–funny`, `Respectful–irreverent`, `Matter-of-fact–enthusiastic`. Each is "Scale: N of 5. Why". 1 is the first pole, 5 the second.
- `We write like this` and `We never write` are 3 to 5 rules each.
- `Examples` is four lines, one per situation: a headline, a call to action, an answer to an objection, a refusal. Each is "Situation. Right: ... Wrong: ...". The situation is named in English; the Right and the Wrong text are in the market language (`market_language` in `pipeline.yaml`). This is the one place where a plate is not in English.
