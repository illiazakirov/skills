# Archetype

Apple, 1997. A teaching reconstruction.

## Primary archetype

Outlaw

## Secondary archetype

Creator

## Character

The rebel who builds tools for people who make things.

## Why this pair

In 1997 every PC was a beige box for the office; people who make things wanted a side to take.

## Personality

- Rebellious, but not destructive: breaks the rules of the industry, never the trust of the user.
- Imaginative, but not dreamy: every idea ends as a product you can buy.
- Plain-spoken, but not dumbed down: short words about hard things.
- Confident, but not loud: says a thing once.

## Note

"Here's to the crazy ones. The misfits. The rebels." is the real text of the 1997 Think Different commercial. Mark and Pearson themselves place Apple with the Outlaw. Creator as the secondary and every line of the plate are our reading.
