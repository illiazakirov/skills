# Motto

Apple, 1997. A teaching reconstruction.

## Motto

Think different.

## What it says

Apple is for people who do not think the way everyone else does.

## Where it stands

The closing line of the film and of each poster, alone on its line; the apple stands apart.

## Rejected

- Think differently. - correct grammar, and the meaning is gone: "different" is what to think, not how

## Note

Real: the campaign first aired on 28 September 1997, made by TBWA\Chiat\Day; the line is by the art director Craig Tanimoto, not by Jobs. Jobs defended the grammar: "'Think differently' wouldn't hit the meaning for me" (Isaacson, "Steve Jobs", 2011). Apple used the line until 2002. "What it says" and "Where it stands" are ours.
