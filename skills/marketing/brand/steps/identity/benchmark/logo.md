# Logo

Apple, 1998. A teaching reconstruction.

## Logo idea

The same apple, without the stripes: the shape alone is enough.

## Kind

Symbol and wordmark

## Construction

An apple silhouette with a bite out of the right side and a detached leaf, in one solid color.

## Minimum size

- Full logo: 24 px high
- Mark: 16 px high

## Clear space

A quarter of the apple's height on every side.

## Never

- More than one color at a time
- Redrawn, stretched or tilted
- On a background that swallows the silhouette

## Note

Real: the shape is Rob Janoff's of 1977 and did not change; Jobs decided to drop the six stripes in late 1997 and the one-color apple appeared on products in 1998; he told Time in May 1998, "The shape is our swoosh." By then the apple usually stood without the company name, which a new brand cannot afford yet. The wording of the idea, the sizes, the clear space and the three rules are ours.
