# Color

Apple, 1998. A teaching reconstruction.

## Color idea

White, and one color you can see through.

## Palette

- White - #FFFFFF - background on light, text on dark
- Black - #000000 - text on light, background on dark
- Bondi Blue - #0095B6 - accent

## Accent is for

The product, and one detail on the page that points to it.

## Accent is never for

Body text, backgrounds, or a second accent beside it.

## Contrast

- Black on White: 21 to 1
- White on Black: 21 to 1
- Bondi Blue on White: 3.5 to 1, large text only
- Bondi Blue on Black: 6 to 1

## Note

Real: the first iMac, announced on 6 May 1998, was white and a translucent blue that Apple called Bondi Blue; "Sorry, no beige." was a line of its advertising; five more colors followed in January 1999. Apple published no hex value, so the one here is an approximation. The dark theme, the wording of the idea and the two accent rules are ours.
