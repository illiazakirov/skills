# Logo

Fixed text of session 1 of 4 of the identity. Show `Briefing` word for word; ask the `Dig` questions as written; show the `Sharpness tests` as a checklist; say the `Link` as written.

## Briefing

**Strategist:** Session 1 of 4: the **Logo**.

**Where it comes from.** Paul Rand, who drew the marks of IBM, UPS and ABC, wrote it down in "Logos, Flags, and Escutcheons", 1991: "A logo doesn't sell (directly), it identifies."

**The idea.** A logo is a signature, not a picture of what the company does. It takes its meaning from the brand behind it, never the other way around. So it has one job: to be recognized, at any size, in one color, in a second.

**Why now.** The platform said who the brand is and how it feels. The logo is the first thing drawn inside that fence, and everything else will stand beside it.

**What we will leave with.** The logo in four files, its rules, and the headline: the **Logo idea**, one line on what the form says.

From here the Art Director leads.

## Dig

1. Where must the logo work at its smallest: a browser tab, an app icon, a label, a stamp?
2. Is there a symbol, a letter or an image you already see when you think of the brand? And one it must never be?
3. There are three kinds: the name alone, a symbol with the name, a monogram. Do you rule any out before I draw?

## Sharpness tests

- The mark is still itself at 24 pixels high.
- It works in one color, with no gradient and no effect.
- You could draw it from memory after five seconds.
- With the name covered, it could not be taken for a competitor from Positioning.
- The Logo idea follows from the Character.

## Link

Next: Color. The logo is black and white on purpose; now it gets the colors it will live in.

## Notes for the agency

- The Strategist gives the briefing and the benchmark. From the `Dig` on, the Art Director leads and signs once: `**Art Director:**`.
- The Dig has three questions. Skip one that the Creative Brief or its `Given` already answers: state your reading in one line.
- The minting of this session is the choice of a logo on a choice page, as the skill describes; there is no Copywriter here. When the logo is fixed, the Art Director proposes the `Logo idea` and the other slots in words, and the CEO accepts or edits them.
- The logo is drawn in black and white. It takes the brand's colors after the Color session.
- `Kind` is one of `Wordmark`, `Symbol and wordmark`, `Monogram`.
- `Minimum size` is two lines, `Full logo: N px high` and `Mark: N px high`, read off the logo sheet: the smallest size at which each is still itself.
- `Clear space` is one line, in units of the mark. `Never` is exactly three lines.
- `Logo idea` is at most 12 words; `Construction` at most 20.
- The headline slot is `Logo idea`.
