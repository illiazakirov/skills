## Briefing

**Strategist:** Session 1 of 7: the **Golden Circle**.

**Where it comes from.** Simon Sinek, 2009: the TED talk "How great leaders inspire action" and the book *Start with Why*.

**The idea.** Picture three rings. The outer one is WHAT you make. The middle one is HOW you do it. The center is WHY you do it at all. Most companies talk from the outside in and never reach the center. The brands people stay loyal to talk from the inside out. Sinek's line: "People don't buy what you do; they buy why you do it."

**Why we start here.** Every later session takes its direction from this plate. Positioning, personality, voice and logo are all ways of expressing the WHY. If the center is vague, everything built on it is decoration.

**What we will leave with.** Three answers, short enough to engrave:

- **WHY**: one belief, one sentence, starting "We believe".
- **HOW**: three principles, each starting with a verb.
- **WHAT**: one plain line a stranger understands.

## Dig

**Why**

1. What is wrong with the way things are done in your field that you cannot stop noticing?
2. When did you first run into it? Name the event, not the feeling.
3. What do you believe that most people in your field would argue with?
4. If this brand fully succeeded, what would be different for the people it serves?

**How**

5. Which rule do you follow even when it costs you time or money?
6. What do you refuse to do, even when clients ask for it?

**What**

7. Explain what you sell to a twelve-year-old.

Depth rule: when an answer sounds like a slogan or is about the product, ask "And why does that matter to you?" Ask it up to three times in a row, never more.

## Sharpness tests

**Why**

- Your nearest competitor would not put their name under it.
- It names neither your product nor money.
- You would say it out loud to a stranger without wincing.

**How**

- Each line is something you do, not something you are.
- For each line you can name one decision it has already made for you.

**What**

- A stranger understands it without a follow-up question.
