## Briefing

**Strategist:** Session 7 of 7: the **Messaging Hierarchy**.

**Where it comes from.** Agency and public-relations practice, often drawn as a "message house"; it has no single author.

**The idea.** One promise at the top. Beneath it, the proofs that make it believable. Beneath those, the honest reasons a good customer still says no, each with your answer.

**Why now.** This is the last plate, and the first one the outside world will read: the website, the pitch and the bio are all cut from it.

**What we will leave with.** One **main promise**, three proofs, three objections.

## Dig

1. Finish the sentence a customer would say to a friend: "Go to them, because ...".
2. Which three facts would you put on the table in front of a skeptic?
3. Why does someone who fits perfectly still say no?
4. What do you answer, truthfully?

## Sharpness tests

- The customer from session 3 would repeat the promise in their own words.
- The promise is something they get, not something you have.
- Every proof rests on a fact a stranger can check.
- Every objection is one you have really heard.
