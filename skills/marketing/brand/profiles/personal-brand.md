# Profile: personal-brand

Status: untested

A personal brand: one person is the brand. What is sold may be services, content, products or a mix; in every case people buy because of who is behind it.

The format of this file is described in `profiles/README.md`.

## Strategy questions

Asked by `brand strategy` in the Dig phase of each session, after the tool's own questions.

### Golden Circle

- What in your own history makes you the person to do this? Name the events, not the traits.
- What would you keep doing on this subject if nobody paid for it?
- What do you refuse to do or to say, even when it would bring an audience?

### Lean Canvas

- What can people pay you for today, and what do you want them to pay for in two years?
- How many hours a week does the brand get, and what else competes for them?
- Which part of the work cannot be done by anyone but you?

### ICP and Jobs-to-be-Done

- Who has already come to you for this, unprompted? Describe two or three real people.
- What were they trying to get done when they found you, and what had they tried before?
- Whose attention do you want that you do not have yet: clients, employers, peers, an audience?

### Positioning

- Who else does this audience follow, read or hire for the same thing? Name people, not companies.
- What do you do, know or show that those people do not?
- What are you known for now, and what do you want to be known for? Where do the two differ?

### Brand wheel

- How do people who worked with you describe you to others? Use their words.
- Which of your traits should the brand show, and which stay private?
- Where is the line between you and the brand: what happens to the brand if you change field?

### Marketing mix

- People: is it only you, or do others appear under the brand (co-authors, guests, a team)?
- Process: how does someone go from first seeing you to working with you or buying from you?
- Physical evidence: what can a stranger check in five minutes that proves you are what you say (work shown, results, names, numbers)?
- Price: what do you charge for now, how is the price set, and what is deliberately free?

### Messaging hierarchy

- Finish the sentence a follower would say to a friend: "Follow them, because ...".
- Which proof is strongest: results, experience, the work itself, or other people's words?
- Why would a person who fits the ICP still not follow or hire you?

## Marketing mix

7P. `marketing-mix.md` holds `Product`, `Price`, `Place`, `Promotion`, `People`, `Process` and `Physical evidence`.

## Platform questions

Asked by `brand brand-platform` in the Dig phase of each session, after the tool's own questions.

### Archetype

- Where does the brand's character differ from your own? What of you stays private?
- Which role do people already give you: teacher, peer, provocateur, guide?

### Voice

- Does the brand say "I" or "we"?
- How do you talk on stage or on a call, and should the writing sound the same?

### Name

- Does the brand carry your own name? If it does, what happens when you want to sell it or step back?

### Mood

- Will your face be part of the brand? In photographs, drawn, or not at all?

Name candidates: the Copywriter adds a fourth approach, the person's own name, so a round is twelve candidates.

## Identity questions

Asked by `brand identity`: the question under `Logo` in the Dig phase of the Logo session, after the tool's own questions; the question under `Surfaces` before the surfaces are built.

### Logo

- Is the logo your own name, your initials, or a mark apart from you?

### Surfaces

- On the avatar: your photograph, or the mark?
